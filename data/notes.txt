COMPANY NOTES — Demo knowledge base for the research agent

Refund Policy:
Customers can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase if the product has not been heavily used.
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.
To start a refund, email support@example.com with the order ID.

Support Hours:
Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM IST.
Weekend support is limited to urgent production issues only.

Product Tiers:
- Starter: up to 3 users, email support
- Growth: up to 20 users, chat + email support
- Enterprise: unlimited users, dedicated success manager

API Rate Limits:
Starter: 100 requests/minute
Growth: 1,000 requests/minute
Enterprise: custom limits

Onboarding:
New customers receive a welcome email within 1 hour of signup.
A setup call can be booked from the dashboard under Settings > Onboarding.
